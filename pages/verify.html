SHA-256 Hashes

Three separate hashes — garment production spec, artwork master file, and metadata JSON. Generated at release. Immutable.

Creator Mark

Ties the artifact to its originator. Format: Studio / Mark-ID. Registered at drop time. Cannot be retroactively applied.

Release Date

ISO 8601 date when hashes were committed to the registry. Items appearing before this date are not authenticated editions.

Claimed Marks

All brand identifiers and phrases claimed in connection with this artifact. Unauthorized use constitutes trademark infringement.
